Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version. Unhiss cleans your recordings on your phone. Your audio and video files, and their names, are never uploaded and never reach us. There is no account and no login. The app sends only three things over the internet: anonymous crash reports, anonymous usage statistics (only with your consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and with an off switch everywhere), and the status of your purchase. None of it is sold or used for advertising.

1. Who we are

Unhiss ("the app") is made by Artem Radchenko, an individual developer based in Poland ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: support@unhiss.app.

This policy covers the Unhiss apps for iPhone, iPad and Android and the website unhiss.app.

2. Your recordings stay on your device

Noise removal runs entirely on your phone or tablet, using an on-device model. The audio and video files you open, the cleaned results and their file names and paths stay on your device. We do not receive, store or have access to them. Cleaned files are saved only where you choose to save or share them.

To open a file, the app asks your system for access to that file (through the file picker, the photo picker or the share menu). It does not scan your library, and it does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or location.

3. What is collected, and why

The app uses three service providers. Each one receives only what is listed below.

Payments themselves are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, email, card or billing details. Apple and Google process payment data as independent controllers under their own privacy policies.

We do not use advertising, advertising identifiers (IDFA, Google Advertising ID), tracking across other companies' apps or websites, or data brokers. On iOS the app does not ask for tracking permission because it does not track you.

Your choices in the app

  • Settings > Privacy > Share usage statistics turns PostHog statistics on or off. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland it is off until you agree on the first screen that asks.
  • Settings > Privacy > Send crash reports turns Sentry crash reports on or off.
  • Purchase checks through RevenueCat cannot be turned off, because they are how the app knows what you bought. They happen only when the app checks or restores a purchase, or you buy one.

4. Your Privacy ID

Because there is no account, we cannot tell who you are. The app shows a random Privacy ID in Settings > Privacy. It is the identifier attached to your usage statistics. Quote it when you ask us to access or delete your data, so we can find the right records. Reinstalling the app creates a new Privacy ID.

5. How long data is kept

  • Crash reports (Sentry): up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Usage statistics (PostHog): up to 12 months, then deleted. We may keep aggregated counts that no longer relate to any Privacy ID.
  • Purchase records (RevenueCat): for as long as needed to provide and restore your purchase, and afterwards as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to answer you, and no longer than 2 years after the last message.

6. Processors and where data is stored

  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): data stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany, de.sentry.io).
  • PostHog (PostHog, Inc.): PostHog Cloud EU, data stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.): data processed in the United States.

Each provider processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Where data is transferred to or accessible from outside the European Economic Area (RevenueCat, and support access by the US parent companies of Sentry and PostHog), the transfer relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

7. Children

Unhiss is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age for consent to online services in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). The app collects no names, email addresses, photos or contact details from anyone. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, for example in a support email, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR or California law), you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • restrict or object to its processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (turn off the switch in Settings); this does not affect processing before you withdrew;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • not be discriminated against for using these rights.

How to use them: email support@unhiss.app with your Privacy ID (Settings > Privacy) and what you want us to do. For a purchase-related request, also tell us the store (App Store or Google Play) and, if you have it, the order or transaction ID from your store receipt. We answer within one month. We do not ask for more information than we need to find your records.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl); you may also contact the authority where you live or work.

9. Security

All data sent by the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to the services' dashboards is limited to the developer and protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication where available.

10. The website

unhiss.app is a static information site with no accounts and no advertising or tracking cookies. Like any web server, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for a short time for security.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app. If a change needs your consent, we will ask for it.
