Support

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Need help? Email support@unhiss.app.

Tell us your phone model, the app version (Settings) and what happened. We usually reply within two working days.

Purchases

How do I restore Unhiss Full on a new phone or after reinstalling?
  1. Make sure the phone is signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.
  2. Open Unhiss and go to Settings.
  3. Under Purchases, tap Restore purchases in Settings.

Full unlocks within a few seconds. You don't need an account with us. A purchase made on iPhone can't be restored on Android, or the other way round, because Apple and Google keep separate records.

Can my family use my purchase?

On iPhone and iPad, yes: Unhiss Full supports Apple Family Sharing. Family members open Unhiss and tap Restore purchases in Settings. Google Play family sharing is not available for this purchase.

Is there a subscription or a trial?

No. The app is free, and Unhiss Full is a single payment. Nothing renews and there is no trial that turns into a plan.

I paid but Full is still locked.

Check your internet connection and tap Restore purchases in Settings. If it is still locked, email us with your store order ID (App Store: the receipt email from Apple; Google Play: the order number starting with "GPA.") and we will sort it out.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or Google Play refund requests. If the feature you paid for doesn't work, tell us too so we can fix it.

Using Unhiss

Which files can I open?

Common audio files, such as M4A, MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC and OGG. Tap Pick audio file in the app. Opening video files and sharing files to Unhiss from other apps are coming in a later version.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Cleaning runs on your phone and works offline. A connection is needed only to buy or restore Unhiss Full.

What can't it remove?

Unhiss removes background noise: wind, traffic, hum, hiss, rain, fans and similar sounds. It does not remove room echo, separate a voice from music, or quieten other people talking. Background music is treated as noise. If the voice sounds thin or words are cut, lower the strength and clean again.

Where are my cleaned files saved?

Wherever you choose when you tap Save or Share. Your original file is never changed.

Privacy

Are my recordings uploaded?

No. Your recordings and their names stay on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for the small amount of anonymous data the app sends.

Where do I find my Privacy ID?

Open Settings and look under Privacy: the Privacy ID row shows an ID you can copy. Because Unhiss has no accounts, this code is how we find the anonymous usage data from your phone. Include it when you email us a privacy request. Reinstalling the app creates a new ID.

How do I delete my data or turn off statistics?

Turn off Usage statistics and Crash reports in Settings > Privacy to stop sending them. To delete what was already sent, email support@unhiss.app with your Privacy ID and the subject "Delete my data". We reply within one month.

Still stuck?

Email support@unhiss.app. We read every message.
